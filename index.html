~ The Epic of the Chthonic Kitchen ~

Odysseus, The Potassium Hoplite

A battle-hardened veteran of the ten-year Trojan Food Fight, Odysseus seeks only to return to the sacred, sunlit Fruit Bowl of Ithaca. Cast into the abyssal depths of Tartarus by the wrath of the culinary gods, he must rely on his razor-sharp peel-dash and unyielding spirit. His rind may bruise, but his resolve never yields.

The Obsidian Cyclops

Once noble guardians of the pantry, these horrors have been corrupted by the dark rot of the Underworld. Encased in a jagged, volcanic crust, their singular, unblinking crimson eyes seek out fresh produce to incinerate in the eternal ovens of the deep.

Charybdis, The Abyssal Maw

The great mechanical terror of the Stygian Sink. A vortex of grinding steel blades and churning dark waters, Charybdis pulls all who wander too close into its inescapable metallic depths. It stands as the final, grueling barrier between Odysseus and the upper realms.
