The Epic of the Chthonic Kitchen

Odysseus, The Potassium Hoplite

Veteran of the ten-year Trojan Food Fight, Odysseus seeks only to return to the sacred Fruit Bowl of Ithaca. Cast into the abyssal depths of Tartarus, he must rely on his razor-sharp peel-dash and unyielding bronze spirit. His flesh may bruise, but his resolve never yields.

The Obsidian Cyclops

Once noble guardians of the pantry, these horrors have been corrupted by the dark rot of the Underworld. Encased in jagged, volcanic crust, their singular, unblinking crimson eyes seek out fresh produce to incinerate in the eternal ovens.

Charybdis, The Abyssal Maw

The great mechanical terror of the deep sink. A vortex of grinding blades and churning dark waters, Charybdis pulls all who wander too close into its inescapable metallic depths. It is the final barrier between Odysseus and the upper realms.
